Same as Apple Cinnamon fruit leather, except don't use cinnamon, and replace some apple sauce that goes in the blender with blueberries (or whatever kind of berries you prefer) instead.  All the kinds of fruit leather require some apple sauce and some of another berry.  A ratio of 2:1 or 3:1 is good for applesauce:other fruit (raspberry, strawberry, or blueberry).

1 bunch apples
1 bit cinammon
1 bunch maple syrup

-First make applesauce: Cut up the apples into small pieces, then put them in the instant pot with a bit of water for 5 minutes.  (Can also boil them on the lowest heat with a bit of water in the bottom in a huge pot, but that takes an hour or more and risks them getting burnt on, so the instant pot is way easier).
-Can use either a dehydrator, or silicone sheets in the oven with the oven on "Dehydrate" mode.  Get the sheets ready that the sauce will be poured onto.
-Put half a blenderful of applesauce in the blender.
-Add 1/2 tsp of cinnamon and a spoonful of maple syrup.
-Blend on medium for about 15 seconds.
-Pour onto the middle of the sheet
-Smooth out with a spatula
-Put in the dehydrator / oven
-Repeat for each sheet
-Dehydrate for about 8-12 hours, depending on temperature.  When they're done you'll be able to slowly peel them off without anything sticky being left.

vegetarian_chili
vegetable:1
meat:1
yield (meals * people):6
prep.:30
cook:90
recipe source: Mom, who got it from Angie, but I modified it

1 c black beans
1 c kidney beans
2 c tomato sauce
1.5 c water (use 2 c if using instant pot and/or tomatoes instead of tomato sauce)
2 # onions
3 cloves garlic
2 # green peppers
2 c corn
1 tsp cumin
1 tbsp chili powder (but much less if it's the spicier kind of chili powder, such as "chili powder ancho" at Bulk Barn)
1 T hot pepper sauce OR
0.25 tsp cayenne
1 bit salt
2 tbsp maple syrup

Soak beans overnight if using dried beans, and cook them for an hour in pressure cooker
Chop the vegetables, adding them to instant pot
Add everything EXCEPT CHEESE, and maybe except tomato sauce, to instant pot and cook for 15 min.  It seems fine to add tomatoes at this point if using tomatoes.
Add the cheese

Note: Original recipe had tomatoes instead of tomato sauce, and cheese.
